This is the design for the primary mod that we're working on. Name: Rabotnik (the workers are called Rabotniks)

I want to be able to have "population" in a game beyond actual players. I don't want actual NPCs wandering around but instead virtual workers that occupy rental living spaces, consume food for calories, and supply labor to workbenches. Workers will try to find a work party asking for labor in the same settlement, attempt to purchase a skill scroll or assume one of the base skills, then purchase food with enough calories to perform it. A citizen can have only one skill. Instead of gaining xp, their rate of xp gain (effectively their quality of life) determines the level at which they can do their skill.

We want this to have a modest impact on performance.
It should at least interact with the legal, tax, rent, settlement, shop, and contract systems.
We need some way to get a new worker started. I propose tying workers to a piece of furniture that allows players to easily grant them money, food, skill scrolls, etc. This also lets players control the number of new workers. By default the worker will try to rent in the building their furniture piece is placed in but if it isn't available they'll look elsewhere in the settlement. For now, workers don't interact with anything outside their settlement and can only be active when placed inside one.
Workers must have a place to live in order to work.
As much as possible workers should have the properties of a player so they can interact with the same systems in the same way.
Workers are part of a default demographic unique to them for the purpose of laws.
